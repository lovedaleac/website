Sports, Houses and Activities

All-round development through physical education, cultural programmes and inter-house participation.

Physical Education, Sports & Games

Each year the school organizes an Annual Sports Meet to encourage stamina, vigor and sportsmanship. The Sports Shield is awarded to the best house.

The school has a large playground and provides opportunities for cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, throw ball, handball, badminton and table tennis under experienced physical education teachers.

Physical Training is compulsory for all students.

House System

Every student is allotted one of four houses: Wisdom, Peace, Charity and Courage. House Captains and Vice Captains lead activities with support from senior and trained teaching staff.

Extra Curricular Activities

Activities like singing, dancing, art and craft, indoor games and outdoor games are part of the school experience.

Talent Development

Students are encouraged to identify and develop talents in quizzing, computers, elocution, oil painting, yoga, karate and more.

Recognition

Awards & Recognition

Students are recognised for excellence in academics, sports and cultural events.

Annual Prize Day and Sports Day are the main felicitation events. Toppers for ICSE and ISC Board Examination are encouraged with scholarships, trophies and medals.
