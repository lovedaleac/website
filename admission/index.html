Admission

Admission Procedure

Prospectus and registration forms can be obtained from the school office.

How to apply

The duly completed form must be submitted to the office at Lovedale Academy on or before the last date stipulated.

  • One certified true copy of the school report for the last two years.
  • One certified copy of the child's birth certificate, Aadhar card and transfer certificate, where applicable.
  • Original documents should be made available upon request whenever a photocopy is submitted.

Please Note: Registration does not entitle the child to admission automatically. The final decision of selection for admission test rests with the school Principal or representative.

Registration window

Issue of prospectus and registration form for Play Group to Class IX commences from 1st February, 9.00 am to 1.00 pm.

Age criteria

The age limit for admission to Nursery/LKG is between 3 and 4 years as on 31st March of the year in which admission is sought.

Medium of instruction

English

Home Work

Parents and guardians are required to assist and guide the child to complete homework and sign the homework diary each day.

Admission Enquiry

For admission questions, email the school office. Include the child's name, class sought, parent contact number and any current school details.
