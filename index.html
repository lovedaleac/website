The School

Founded in 1992 at Turner Road, Clement Town

Lovedale Academy is affiliated to the Council for Indian School Certificate Examinations, New Delhi. The school began as a co-ed preparatory school and has grown under the Society for the Advancement of Education.

Today the school conducts ICSE (Class X) and ISC (Class XII) board examinations in Science and Commerce streams, and is known for result-oriented academics and value-based education.
