Academics

Academic Programmes

A progressive school journey from early learning to ICSE and ISC board preparation.

Pre-Primary School Program

In the Junior and Kindergarten years, emphasis is placed equally on children's intellectual, physical and social development. Children learn through games, songs, creative craft work, stories, role play and early numeracy activities.

Primary School Program

In classes I to IV, the focus is on inquiry, exploration, confidence, reading, writing, listening, speaking, computer knowledge and problem solving.

Middle School Program

Classes V to VII build on earlier foundations. Students meet new challenges as reasoning, problem solving and value judgement become more complex.

Secondary School Program

In Class VIII, students prepare for the ICSE curriculum and are guided for course choices in Classes IX and X.

Senior Secondary and CISCE

In Classes XI and XII, students prepare for life. These two foundation years support future study and careers with dependable academic preparation.

The Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations administers the ICSE and ISC examinations. Students take productive work and community service as part of the programme.

Examinations / Assessments

All classes from UKG to X have two exams. Final progress is assessed on day-to-day work, regular tests and marks added to half-yearly and final examinations. Classes X and XII have preliminary examinations in November/December and January.

Promotion

A child has to pass in all subjects in order to earn promotion.

School Timings

The school functions from Monday to Saturday, six days per week. For current timing details, please contact the school office.
